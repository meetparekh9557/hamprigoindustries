# Photos

Put photographs for the website in this folder.

## How to upload

1. Go to this folder on GitHub:
   https://github.com/meetparekh9557/hamprigoindustries/tree/main/public/photos
2. Click **Add file**, then **Upload files**.
3. Drag the photos in, or click **choose your files** and pick them.
4. Scroll down and click **Commit changes**.

That is all. You do not need to rename, resize or compress anything first.

## What happens after you upload

Uploading a photo does not put it on the website by itself. It only puts the
file where the website can reach it. Tell me which photos you have added and
where they should appear, and I will place them on the page, size them for the
web, and add the alt text that search engines and screen readers read.

Photos straight from a phone or a camera are usually three or four megabytes
each, which is far too heavy for a web page. That is fine. Upload them at full
size and I will produce the smaller versions the site actually loads. Do not
try to shrink them yourself, because once detail is lost it cannot be
recovered.

## Naming

Anything readable is fine. Names like `lamination-machine-1.jpg` or
`factory-floor.jpg` are easier to talk about than `IMG_20260904_113245.jpg`,
but if renaming is a nuisance, upload them as they are and tell me what each
one shows.

Avoid spaces in file names. Use a hyphen instead.

## The logo

The original logo artwork can go in here too. Name it `logo` plus whatever
extension it has: `logo.svg`, `logo.ai`, `logo.eps` or `logo.png`.

An `.svg`, `.ai` or `.eps` is the most useful, because those stay sharp at any
size. The mark currently on the site is a reconstruction I drew from the scan
you sent, not the original artwork, so if the real file exists it should
replace it.

## What not to put here

Anything in this folder is published on the public website, and a search engine
can find it whether or not a page links to it. So keep it to photographs and
artwork meant for the site. Price lists, customer details, contracts and
internal documents do not belong here.
